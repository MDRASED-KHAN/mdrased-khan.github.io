About

I am a PhD researcher in Applied Biology & Biotechnology at RMIT University, Australia, working in the Neurotoxicology Laboratory. My doctoral research explores how animals without complex brains learn, adapt and make decisions, using shrimps as a model for behavioural plasticity and proto-cognitive traits.

Before joining RMIT, I trained in fisheries science at Patuakhali Science and Technology University, Bangladesh, where my work focused on microplastic pollution in aquaculture and coastal fishes, fish population biology, and the fishery resources of Bangladesh's rivers.
